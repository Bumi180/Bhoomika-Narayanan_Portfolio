About the
collector

I'm a Tamil Canadian designer based between Waterloo and Toronto, and a lot of my work starts with where I come from, then widens to the communities around me. I'm a 2nd year GBDA student at the University of Waterloo, somewhere between design, art and business. My brain wants structure, my heart wants free creative work, and most of what I make sits in that gap.

Colour is where I get the pickiest. I draw constantly outside of class, mostly on my art Instagram. Right now I'm leaning toward instructional design and EdTech, where structure and creativity finally point the same way.
